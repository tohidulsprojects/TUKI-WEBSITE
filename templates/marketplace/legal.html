Information we use

We use the account, contact, optional profile photo, delivery, order, and payment-submission details you provide to operate the marketplace, deliver purchases, prevent fraud, and respond to support requests. Profile photos are stored privately and served only to the signed-in account that uploaded them.

Payment data

Manual wallet payments are verified using the selected provider, sender number, transaction ID, and optional receipt. TUKISHOP never asks for wallet PINs or OTPs. Do not send those details to anyone.

Access and retention

Only authorized administrators and relevant sellers can see information required to process an order. We retain records as needed for service, accounting, and legal requirements.

Contact

Contact the support number shown on the website to ask about your data.

Marketplace orders

Product availability, seller fulfillment, delivery time, shipping fees, and promotions may vary by seller and destination. The final amount is shown before an order is placed.

Manual payments

For bKash, Nagad, Upay, and Rocket, follow the number and instructions shown on your order page. An order is not marked paid until an administrator verifies the transaction. Never share your PIN or OTP.

Returns and cancellations

Cancellation requests are reviewed based on order progress. Refund eligibility and timing depend on the order, seller, and payment status. Contact support with your order number.

Seller terms

Sellers must provide accurate listings, maintain stock, fulfill accepted orders, and follow marketplace policies. TUKISHOP may moderate or suspend listings and seller accounts.
